5 個 FIX 的處置

A4 §B.3——Explicit they already are 是倒裝，asks for them explicit 對 EFL 偏生硬。

前Strategy B.1 asks for them explicit and in order. Explicit they already are. But English likes to trail a for all behind the clause it governs, so the last of the three has ended up sitting behind the kernel instead of in front of it.
後Strategy B.1 requires the quantifiers to be explicit and in order. They are already explicit. But English often places a for all after the clause it governs, so the last of the three appears after the kernel rather than before it.

A8 §B.4——must come to less 缺明說的比較基準（less than what？）。

前The split into thirds set three allowances of \(\varepsilon/3\), which together come to exactly \(\varepsilon\), so the three pieces they cover must come to less.
後The split into thirds gave each of the three differences an allowance of \(\varepsilon/3\). Since those allowances add to exactly \(\varepsilon\), the three differences together come to less than \(\varepsilon\).

A13 §B.6——我拆掉兩個破折號後，Short of …, and over the integers … 的插入語句法鬆脫。

前Short of checking every object the claim ranges over, and over the integers you never can, no number of confirming examples establishes a for all.
後Over the integers, no number of confirming examples establishes a for all, because you can never check every object in its range.

A15 §B.6——44 詞冒號句仍同時承載勸告、替代行為與後果。（第二輪原本只改了慣用語、沒拆。）

前You can plan a proof before you can prove it, and should: the usual alternative is to start writing at the top of the page without a plan, which often leads to using at line one a fact that is only established at line ten.
後You can plan a proof before you can prove it, and you should. If you start writing at the top of the page without a plan, you may use at line one a fact that is only established at line ten.

B3 §B.6——我拆過頭了：三個短宣告連續出現，正是 Q3 指出「讀起來像節拍器」的那一處。合回為一個單一複雜想法句。

前The indirectness of a contraposition is in what it establishes, not in how. You never argued about the original claim itself. You proved an equivalent sentence, and §B.1 carried the result across.
後Proof by contraposition is indirect only in what it establishes, not in how: you prove an equivalent sentence rather than the original claim, and §B.1 carries the result across.

W-13(a)：on credit 政策——保留機制用語、只改周邊

前We take the theorem on credit and use what it grants: … This is the honest boundary of the chapter — everything else is earned in full.
後We take the theorem on credit and use what it gives us: … Everything else in this chapter is proved in full.

執行時的發現，讓 (a) 更省：全書「on credit」共 5 處（ch02 §2.4 首見、ch04 §4.1×2、appD §D.1、ch06 §6.2、ch06 §6.5），而首見處早在 2026-07-03 就 gloss 過了——fragment 註解記載 "on credit" idiom glossed (non-native stumble)，是初學者模擬（learner-sim）抓到非母語卡點後補的：“Everything about \(e^{x}\) in this section is on credit — borrowed now, to be repaid in Chapter 4.” ch04 §4.1 接著寫 “goes back and pays the debt”、appD §D.1 寫 “deferred — on credit — to here”。這是全書刻意貫穿、首見已釋義的機制隱喻，故不需新增 gloss。
