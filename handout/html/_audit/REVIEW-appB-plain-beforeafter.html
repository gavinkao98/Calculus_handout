§1 病灶分五類，先看最有代表性的

每組：改前 → 改後，上方一行說明為什麼。標示色塊是實際變動處。

① 英語慣用語／俚語（EFL 讀者查字典也拼不出意思）

lockstep（軍隊齊步）＝「完全同步」。改後直接說出數學關係。 §B.1

改前Its contrapositive “if n is not divisible by 3, then n is not divisible by 6” is true again, in lockstep with the original.
改後Its contrapositive “if n is not divisible by 3, then n is not divisible by 6” is true again, exactly when the original statement is true.

help yourself to（擅自取用）是道地口語，但這裡是 Caution 的承載句——最不該讓讀者猜。 §B.1 Caution

改前The single most common misreading of a theorem is to help yourself to its converse.
改後The single most common misreading of a theorem is to use its converse without proof.

rabbit from a hat（魔術戲法）是文化綁定的比喻——而且本節標題 Steps That Come From Nowhere 早就給了現成的字面說法。 §B.4

改前The step δ = min{δ₁, δ₂} reads like a rabbit from a hat the first time, but it says only this: …
改後The step δ = min{δ₁, δ₂} can seem to come from nowhere the first time, but it says only this: …

out of the blue（突如其來）→ 說出實際情況：沒有可見的動機。 §B.4

改前…a proof will often introduce h = f − g out of the blue and argue about h alone…
改後…a proof will often introduce h = f − g with no visible motivation and argue about h alone…

一句裡三個慣用語（nobody's business／by luck／need appear），而且後半跟下一句重複。刪掉慣用語後更短更清楚。 §B.6

改前Where the witness came from is nobody’s business: you may have found it by trial, by symmetry, or by luck, and none of that need appear.
改後You may have found the witness by trial, by symmetry, or by luck; none of that needs to appear.

三個慣用語擠在同一段（parts company with、will not crack、without a murmur）。 §B.6 Ex B.11

改前Read the inductive step as closely as you like; it will not crack. … The first and fifth habits of Strategy B.5 pass it without a murmur. … which is where this example parts company with the other two…
改後Read the inductive step as closely as you like; you will find no error in it. … The first and fifth habits of Strategy B.5 find nothing wrong with it. … which is where this example differs from the other two…

② 擬人與隱喻（把數學物件寫成有意志的角色）

量詞被寫成會「自我宣告」的角色。 §B.1

改前They rarely announce themselves.
改後They are often implicit rather than stated explicitly.

量詞「當家作主」＋「重新調校」兩個隱喻疊在 Caution 裡。改後說的是同一件事，但用讀者能直接檢查的語言。 §B.3 Caution

改前When quantifiers of different type meet, the outer one is in charge, and the inner object may be retuned for each choice of the outer.
改後When quantifiers of different type meet, the outer one comes first, and the inner object may be chosen anew for each choice of the outer.

案例被寫成「吃掉一小時」；證明被寫成「養成壞習慣、擅自取用」。 §B.6

改前The false starts, the case that ate an hour before it collapsed, the algebra done three times: none of it belongs on the page, however hard it was won.
改後The false starts, the case analysis that took an hour and then fell apart, the algebra done three times: none of it belongs on the page, however much work it took.

attacking it head-on／laid a finger on（動武比喻）→ 直接說「不從 P 直接推到 Q」。這段在教 contraposition 與 contradiction 的分野，最需要精準。 §B.6

改前Two ways of reaching a claim without attacking it head-on, and it matters that you do not blur them. … you never laid a finger on the original claim, you proved a different sentence and let §B.1 carry the result across.
改後Two ways of proving a claim without arguing directly from P to Q, and it matters that you do not confuse them. … you never argued about the original claim itself; you proved an equivalent sentence, and §B.1 carried the result across.

定義被寫成「有把手」「有門可推」。改後保留格言節奏，但講的是可操作的事。 §B.6 Strategy B.6

改前Definitions are where a proof gets its handles: a word you have not unpacked is a door you have not tried.
改後Definitions make the conditions in a statement usable: a word you have not unpacked is a condition you cannot yet use.

③ 交易隱喻家族（earn／repay／owe／buy）——全書性 tic，共 14 處

單獨看每處都還算透明，累積起來就是「太文學」的主要來源；ch06 §6.2 的 “everything else is earned in full” 是同一家族。

技能被寫成「會回本」。 §B.1

改前Reading the statement closely is the first of these skills, and the one that repays itself most often.
改後Reading the statement closely is the first of these skills, and the one you will use most often.

雙向蘊含被寫成「兩個方向都得掙來」→ 直接說：兩個方向各自要證。 §B.1 Caution

改前…that is a strictly stronger claim, and it has to be earned in both directions.
改後…that is a strictly stronger claim, and each direction must be proved separately.

兩個交易隱喻（buying）＋一個擬人（h「買到」東西）。這節的主題正是「這一步在做什麼」，用字面說反而更貼題。 §B.4，共 3 處

改前…turns into the far smaller “ah, that one again — what is it buying here?” … Notice what h bought, because that is the whole of the move.
改後…turns into the far smaller “ah, that one again — what does it accomplish here?” … Notice what introducing h made possible, because that is the whole of the move.

假設被寫成「掙得它的位置」（earn its place）→ 說出實際要求：被用到。 §B.5 Strategy B.5、§B.6 各一處

改前Every assumption in the statement should earn its place at some specific line. ／ Strategy B.5 taught you to hunt for the line where each hypothesis earns its place…
改後Every assumption in the statement should be used at some specific line. ／ Strategy B.5 taught you to find the line at which each hypothesis is used…

Caution 的收尾句：「還沒掙到的一步」→「還沒證成的一步」。同段另一處 the converse has to be earned → has to be proved。 §B.6 Caution

改前A step you want is a step you have yet to earn.
改後A step you want is a step you have not yet justified.

④ 修辭遮住了數學內容（最該修的一類）

給 EFL 讀者的白話保命句，卻用了兩個運動慣用語（clears the bar、moves the finish line），而且沒說出否定規則到底做了什麼。改後把規則三個動作說全：換量詞、保順序、否定條件。 §B.3 Strategy B.3 白話欄

改前In words: to deny that everyone clears the bar is to claim that someone fails it; to deny that someone succeeds is to claim that everyone fails. Negation trades the two quantifiers and moves the finish line.
改後In words: to negate “every object satisfies a condition” is to claim that at least one does not; to negate “some object satisfies a condition” is to claim that no object does. Negation changes each quantifier to the other type, keeps their order, and negates the final condition.

a shrug（聳肩）承載「不構成矛盾」這個關鍵判斷。改後把它說成可檢查的話。 §B.2

改前…reducing the fraction to lowest terms is precisely what turns “both even” into a contradiction rather than a shrug.
改後…reducing the fraction to lowest terms is precisely what makes “both even” a contradiction; without it, “both even” would contradict nothing.

counterexample 的定義段連用三個比喻（promises／clears some bar／knock it down）。改後用的是本書自己的術語。 §B.2

改前A statement of the form for all promises that every object in its range clears some bar. To knock it down, you need just one object that does not — a counterexample.
改後A statement of the form for all claims that every object in its range satisfies some condition. To disprove it, you need just one object that does not — a counterexample.

「arbitrary」的規矩本來只用「你掙到了嗎」暗示。改後把條件寫出來（不得使用 x 的任何額外性質），並拆成兩句。 §B.6

改前The last line is not a flourish either. It is the step that converts one anonymous case into all of them, and it is worth writing precisely because it reminds you to check that you earned it.
改後The last line is not decoration either. It is the step that converts one arbitrary case into all of them. It is worth writing precisely because it reminds you to check that the argument used nothing about x beyond its membership in S and the hypothesis P(x).

「希望論證在寫到頁底前抵達」是雙重擬人＋慣用語，而真正要講的規矩（第一行不能用第十行才成立的事）藏在後半。 §B.6 Strategy B.6 白話欄

改前…the usual alternative is to start writing at the top of the page and hope the argument arrives before the bottom, which is how proofs pick up their worst habit, helping themselves at line one to what is not earned until line ten.
改後…the usual alternative is to start writing at the top of the page without a plan, which often leads to using at line one a fact that is only established at line ten.

原用語改後為什麼
The tell:（×5：§B.2 三處、§B.4、§B.6）The clue:／To tell them apart, look at…撲克行話（破綻）。原本想用 The sign，但會跟數學的 sign of f′(x) 撞詞，故用 clue。

untenable／relentlessly／stubborn ε／a head start／in two strokes／a wall of text／conjured from thin air／carved into thirds／no direct grip／a close cousin／the odds are strong／Demolishes; never builds.cannot hold／appears so often／one fixed ε／an advantage／in two steps／a long proof／seems to come from nowhere／split into thirds／cannot bound … directly／a close variation／it is very likely／It disproves; it never proves.低頻詞、比喻、電報體，逐一換成中性直白詞（各 1 處）。

§2 哪些沒有改（同樣重要）

條款的設計是「數學內容必須字面化」，不是「禁用一切修辭」。以下明確保留，因為它們旁邊都已有字面說明，且真的幫助理解：

  • 定理＝承諾：“A theorem is a promise, and like any promise it comes with conditions.”——§B.6 教「什麼會讓定理毀約」時靠這個框架承重。
  • 骨牌：“The dominoes are standing in a row … and nobody ever pushed the first.”——前一句已字面寫出「沒有 base case」。
  • ε-δ 攻防賽（挑戰者出 ε、你回 δ）：教學正典，且當場算了一整輪 δ = ε/2。
  • 單行道：“An implication P ⇒ Q is a one-way street.”——同段完整展開。
  • collision（矛盾）：Codex 特別指出我原本想改的 “collides with nothing” 屬於已定錨的家族，改掉反而破壞一致性——我接受這條撤回。
  • “Discovery is allowed to be a mess; presentation is not.”、“Two well-chosen cases beat three thousand well-checked ones.”、“it is a promise to write one”——平實不等於乾癟的示範。
