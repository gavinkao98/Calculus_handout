§2 全附錄級政策裁決（G-1〜G-6）——先裁這六項

編號現象（全部出處已錄）建議
G-1 "The tell" 作名詞（撲克行話「破綻」）共 5 處：B.2 Strategy 三個 bullet（"The tell: an opening like…"×3）、B.4（"The tell is a function…"）、B.6（"The tell, then, is what the outer argument ends with"）。對 EFL 讀者這是不可推測的行話（R1）。 全改為 "The sign to look for"／"The sign:"（5 處機械替換，語意不變）。次選：B.2 首用時 gloss 一次、其餘保留——但 5 處全改更省心智負擔。
G-2 交易隱喻家族 earn／repay／owe／buy／cost／win 共 ≈14 處："repays itself most often"（B.1）、"has to be earned in both directions"（B.1 Caution）、"what is it buying here?"（B.4×3 系）、"earn its place"／"repays every hour"（B.5）、"not earned until line ten"／"it would earn very little"／"the inner skeleton owes a witness"／"the converse has to be earned"／"a step you have yet to earn"（B.6）等。單處多半透明，累積起來是本附錄最大的「文學腔」來源，且與 ch06 §6.2 的 "earned in full" 同一家族（全書性 tic）。 政策：預設改字面（earned → proved／established；owes → must supply；buying → achieving），白名單保留 2 處透明且有 payoff 的："it is a promise to write one"（B.6 witness 段，妙且直白）、B.4 的 buy 系若你認為它一致且已被字面句配好（我方建議：也改，一致性優先）。逐處落點見各節卡。
G-3 "help yourself to / helps itself to"（擅自取用）共 3 處：B.1 Caution（"help yourself to its converse"）、B.6 Strategy informal（"helping themselves at line one…"）、B.6 arbitrary 段（"quietly helps itself to a convenient x"）。慣用語，EFL 不可推測（R1），且都落在承載紀律教學的位置。 全改："use its converse without proof"／"using at line one a fact that is only established at line ten"／"quietly assumes a convenient x"。
G-4 "rabbits from a hat"（魔術戲法）×2：附錄 objectives（"look like rabbits from a hat"）、B.4 Ex B.5（"reads like a rabbit from a hat"）。文化綁定慣用語；且 B.4 節標題本身就是現成的字面說法。 全改為 "seem to come from nowhere"——與節標題 "Steps That Come From Nowhere" 自然呼應，反而更一致。
G-5 "A theorem is a promise" 框架：B.1 開場句、B.6 負向推理教學（"§B.1 called a theorem a promise; ask what would break this one"）、B.5／B.2 的 "promises" 動詞散用。B.6 的否定教學靠這個框架承重（「什麼會毀約」→ P 真而 Q 假）。 保留框架（條件式＝承諾是邏輯教學的常規類比、B.1 當場字面化、B.6 callback 有真教學功能）——這是「條款不是鏈鋸」的示範。僅微調：B.1 開場第二分句 "and like any promise it comes with conditions" 可保留；B.2 的 "promises that every object…clears some bar" 這種散用改字面（見 R-B2-4）。若你要全拆此框架，B.1＋B.6 兩處必須聯動改，工程較大、不建議。
G-6 已固化且有字面配套的 quasi-term 家族——明確 PASS 保護：collision／collide（矛盾的家用語，B.2 首用時字面定義過）、one-way street／travel／carry（蘊含方向，B.5 Caution 完整 cash out）、domino chain（歸納，B.2 字面兩步驟先行）、skeleton／machinery、ε-δ challenge–response contest（B.3，教學正典且有完整實算一輪）。 不動。它們一致使用、每次都有字面說明相鄰，正是 FLAG 紀律要保護的「合法教學隱喻」。列在此防止協調改寫時誤殺。

B.3 A Quantifier Toolbox — 7 卡節級複查：觸發

編號原句（節錄）診斷改寫示意
R-B3-1"a statement's whole meaning can turn on how they are arranged"R1：turn on phrasal（取決於），EFL 易誤讀成「開啟」。a statement's whole meaning can depend entirely on their order

R-B3-5informal: "to deny that everyone clears the bar is to claim that someone fails it… Negation trades the two quantifiers and moves the finish line."R1：白話槽位（informal）反而用了兩個運動慣用語（clears the bar、finish line）——這正是給 EFL 讀者的保命句，必須字面。In words: to deny that every object satisfies the condition is to claim that at least one does not; to deny that some object does is to claim that none do. R-B3-6"One arrangement…recurs so relentlessly that it deserves to be known on sight."R1：relentlessly 文學副詞＋known on sight 慣用。One arrangement…appears so often that you should learn to recognize it at once.
