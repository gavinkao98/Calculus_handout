The Missing Rule

We can differentiate sine and cosine now -- but only in their bare form. We can handle $\sin x$, yet nothing so far reaches $\sin(x^2)$ or $\sqrt $, where one function is fed into another. The Whole Idea, in One Picture

Here's the whole idea in one picture. Near a point, a differentiable function behaves like its tangent line -- it scales a small change in its input by its slope, with an error that shrinks faster than the change does. Now chain two together: $g$ scales a small increment $h$ by $g'(x)$, and then $f$ scales that by $f'(g(x))$. The two magnifications stack -- so the composite scales $h$ by the product $f'(g(x))\,g'(x)$. That's the chain rule; the proof, later, is just checking the two small errors stay negligible once chained.

The Chain Rule

Let's state it precisely. If $g$ is differentiable at $x_0$, and $f$ is differentiable at $g(x_0)$, then the composition $P(x)=f(g(x))$ is differentiable at $x_0$ too. And its derivative is the outer derivative at the inner function, times the inner derivative. Read it from the inside out. We'll earn this with a proof a little later -- for now, let's learn to use it.

Two Stretches in a Row

Picture the rule as a relay of two stretches. Start with a small increment $h$ sitting at $x_0$. The first function $g$ carries it forward and stretches it to about $g'(x_0)\,h$, now sitting at $u_0=g(x_0)$. The second function $f$ stretches that again by its own slope there, landing at about $f'(g(x_0))\,g'(x_0)\,h$. Two stretches one after the other -- and stretching by one factor then another just multiplies the factors. That's why the composite's slope is the product of the two local slopes.

The Leibniz Form

There's a second way to write the rule, and it's the one you'll actually compute with. Name the inner variable $u=g(x)$ and the output $y=f(u)$. 移走的是 $m_2=f'(g(x_0))$ 的定義

耳朵聽到的`proof_setup_substitution` 的旁白只說 “apply its remainder form too”，沒有為 $m_2$ 命名；下一場 `proof_easy_piece` 直接說 “$m_2$ is a constant”。也就是說，$m_2$ 是什麼，耳朵從頭到尾沒聽到。

畫面上有沒有有（A2b 補的）。2026-09-14 把 $m_2=f'(g(x_0))$ 接在 proof.1 那一列的式尾，補上這個「被命名、被當常數用，卻從未在畫面上定義」的缺口。

怎麼判這是本節最需要「耳眼合看」的一處：只聽不看，$m_2$ 會是一個憑空出現的常數。

proof_delicate_bound

移走的是 $\alpha_1$ 這個名字（第二次縮小窗口所取的門檻）

耳朵聽到的旁白改說 “…and shrinking the window once more so the absolute value of the ratio of $R_1(h)$ to $h$ is less than one…”——數學等價，但 $\alpha_1$ 不再被點名。

畫面上有沒有沒有。畫面兩列 proof 也未出現 $\alpha_1$；講義（`chapter3.tex`）有這個名字，影片版兩端都以「再縮一次窗口」的說法代替。

怎麼判對影片聽眾這是簡化，不是缺漏——該步驟的內容（取更小的門檻使 $\lvert R_1(h)/h\rvert < 1$）耳朵聽得到；只是那個希臘字母沒有被引入。若您認為證明的可追溯性需要這個名字，這一場就要改。
