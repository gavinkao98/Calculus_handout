1. Task D-2 — 逐單元對齊 chapter3.tex

方法：把 .tex §3.1 全文（\sechead{3.1} 到 \sechead{3.2}） 與 21 個 content 場的旁白逐單元對讀，每一處分歧判「跟改／不跟改」。5 場跟改：

03 difference_quotient_for_sine — 補和差化積恆等式的來源

前：The decisive step is one trigonometric identity — sum-to-product — which turns a difference of sines into a product. A product is exactly what we want…
後：…into a product. And if it looks like it fell out of the sky, it did not: put $u=\frac{A+B}{2}$ and $v=\frac{A-B}{2}$, so that $A=u+v$ and $B=u-v$; expand $\sin(u+v)-\sin(u-v)$ with the angle-sum formulas, and the $\sin u\cos v$ terms cancel, leaving exactly $2\cos u\sin v$. A product is also exactly what we want…

出處：chapter3.tex:36「If this identity is not familiar, it follows from the angle-sum formulas. Set $u=\frac{A+B}{2}$ and $v=\frac{A-B}{2}$, so that $A=u+v$ and $B=u-v$.」影片原本是「天上掉下來的恆等式」，六鏡也點過。

06 sector_inequality — 補 corner piece $ABC$ 一句

前：…their areas must line up in the same order: $\frac12\sin\theta \le \frac12\theta \le \frac12\tan\theta$. The whole argument hangs on that one picture.
後：…in the same order: … And you can see exactly what each one adds: the corner piece $ABC$ is what the outer triangle adds to the inner one, and inside it sits the sliver between the chord $AB$ and the arc. The whole argument hangs on that one picture.

出處：chapter3.tex:56「Equivalently, split the outer triangle as $OAC = OAB \cup ABC$: the corner piece $ABC$ is what $OAC$ adds to the inscribed triangle $OAB$, and it contains the circular segment between the chord $AB$ and the arc.」這一句正好是視覺 advisory「橘色＝增量 vs 累積」的文字版。

17 derivative_of_cosine — 補伴隨恆等式的來源

前：This time use the companion sum-to-product formula.
後：This time use the companion sum-to-product formula — obtained the same way as the first one, by expanding $\cos(u+v)-\cos(u-v)$.

出處：chapter3.tex:117「obtained just like the sine version by expanding $\cos(u+v)-\cos(u-v)$ with the angle-sum formulas」

23 shm_compute — 正確性：收回過強的宣稱

前：That relation, $s''=-s$, is the signature of simple harmonic motion; sine and cosine are exactly the functions that describe it.
後：…is the signature of simple harmonic motion — and it is the four-step cycle stopped halfway. Both sine and cosine satisfy that relation, so either one can describe an oscillation.

出處：chapter3.tex:191「Both sine and cosine satisfy this relation, so both can be used to describe oscillation.」原句過強：$s''=-s$ 的解空間是 $a\sin t+b\cos t$，說「exactly the functions」不精確。

26 recap — 接收從場 13 移來的度數導數公式

前：…with the fine print that it all depends on radians.
後：…depends on radians: in degrees the derivative of $\sin(x^\circ)$ picks up a factor of $\pi$ over $180$, so the clean $\sin'=\cos$ and $\cos'=-\sin$ hold only in radian measure.

出處：D-1b 的落點：兩條定理都證完之後講，才不是前向參照。
